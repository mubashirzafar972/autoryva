Pontiac GTO

Autoryva Notes

Many people credit the GTO with starting the muscle car era by fitting a large V8 into a mid-size Pontiac. The idea was simple, and the result was fast, loud and popular with young buyers. Genuine early cars are valuable and many look-alikes exist, so check the identification numbers carefully before paying for a real GTO.

From Wikipedia

The Pontiac GTO is a rear-drive, two-door, and four-passenger automobile manufactured and marketed by the Pontiac division of General Motors over four generations from 1963 until 1974 in the United States—with a fifth generation made by GM's subsidiary, Holden, for the 2004 to 2006 model years. The first generation of the GTO is credited with popularizing the muscle car market segment in the 1960s. Some consider the Pontiac GTO to have started the trend with all four domestic automakers offering a variety of competing models.
