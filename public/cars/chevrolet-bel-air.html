Chevrolet Bel Air

Autoryva Notes

The Bel Air became the face of 1950s American style, especially the 1955 to 1957 models with their chrome, bold colours and optional V8. It was sold as an ordinary family car, which is why so many survive and why parts are easy to find. Many owners enjoy modifying them. When buying, check for rust in the lower body and ask about any engine swaps.

From Wikipedia

The Chevrolet Bel Air is a full-size car produced by Chevrolet for the 1950–1981 model years. Initially, only the two-door hardtops in the Chevrolet model range were designated with the Bel Air name from 1950 to 1952. With the 1953 model year, the Bel Air name was changed from a designation for a unique body shape to a premium level of trim applied across a number of body styles.
