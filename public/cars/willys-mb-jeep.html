Willys MB Jeep

The Willys MB ( WIL-iss) and the Ford GPW, both formally called the U.S. Army truck, 1⁄4‑ton, 4×4, command reconnaissance, commonly known as the Willys Jeep, Jeep, or jeep, and sometimes referred to by its Standard Army vehicle supply number G‑503, were highly successful American off-road capable, light military utility vehicles. Over 600,000 were built to a single standardized design for the United States and the Allied forces in World War II, from 1941 to 1945. This also made it the world's first mass-produced four-wheel-drive car, built in six-figure numbers.
