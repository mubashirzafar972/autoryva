Lancia Fulvia

From Wikipedia

The Lancia Fulvia (Tipo 818) is a car produced by Lancia between 1963 and 1976. Named after the Via Fulvia, a Roman road leading from Tortona to Turin, it was introduced at the Geneva Motor Show in 1963 and manufactured in three variants: Berlina 4-door saloon, 2-door Coupé, and Sport, an alternative fastback coupé designed and built by Zagato on the Coupé floorpan. Fulvias are noted for their role in motorsport history, including a 1972 win of the International Rally Championship.
