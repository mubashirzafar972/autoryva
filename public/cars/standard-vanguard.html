Standard Vanguard

From Wikipedia

The Standard Vanguard is a car which was produced by the Standard Motor Company in Coventry, England, from 1947 until 1963. The car, announced in July 1947, was completely new, with no resemblance to previous models. Designed in 1945, it was Standard's first post-World War II car and intended for export around the world.
