Mercedes-Benz 190 SL

From Wikipedia

See Mercedes-Benz SL-Class for a complete overview of all SL-Class models. The Mercedes-Benz 190 SL (W121) is a two-door luxury roadster produced by Mercedes-Benz between May 1955 and February 1963. Internally referred to as W121 (BII), it was first shown in prototype at the 1954 New York Auto Show, and was available with an optional removable hardtop.
