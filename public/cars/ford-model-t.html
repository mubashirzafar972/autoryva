Ford Model T

Autoryva Notes

The Model T is the car that made owning a car normal. Ford’s moving assembly line cut build times and prices, so ordinary families could afford one. It was simple, tall on its wheels and easy to repair with basic tools, which suited the poor roads of its day. At a show, look at the pedals: they work very differently from a modern car, and drivers had to learn them before setting off.

From Wikipedia

The Ford Model T is an automobile that was produced by the Ford Motor Company from October 1, 1908, to May 26, 1927. It is generally regarded as the first mass-affordable automobile, which made car travel available to middle-class Americans. The relatively low price was partly the result of Ford's efficient fabrication, including assembly line production instead of individual handcrafting.
