Austin / Morris Mini

Autoryva Notes

The Mini used a clever layout with the engine turned sideways, which left most of the car for people and luggage. It was tiny, cheap and surprisingly fun to drive, so it became popular with everyone from families to rally drivers. Check the sills, subframes and floors for rust, and remember that a cheap example can hide expensive repairs.

From Wikipedia

The Mini is a two-door, four-seat small car produced for four decades over a single generation, with many names and variants, by the British Motor Corporation (BMC) and its successors British Leyland and the Rover Group, and finally (briefly) under BMW ownership. Minis were built as saloons, estates, convertibles, and various other body styles. Minus a brief 1990s hiatus, from 1959 into 2000, an estimated 5.38 million of all variations combined were built.
