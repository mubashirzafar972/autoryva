Mercedes-Benz W123

Autoryva Notes

The W123 earned a reputation for durability, and many are still working decades after they were built. Its upright shape, comfortable seats and strong diesel options made it a favourite as a taxi and a family car in many countries. Simple mechanics keep running costs fair. Rust in the wheel arches and sills is the main thing to check.

From Wikipedia

The Mercedes-Benz W123 is a range of executive cars produced by German manufacturer Mercedes-Benz from November 1975 to January 1986. The W123 models surpassed their predecessor, the Mercedes-Benz W114, as the most successful Mercedes-Benz, selling 2.7 million units before production ended in the autumn of 1985 for the saloon/sedan versions and January 1986 for coupés and estates/station wagons. Following a slow production build-up during the first year, customers who placed their orders faced a lengthy waiting period of nine to twelve months.
