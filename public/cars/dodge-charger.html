Dodge Charger

Autoryva Notes

The Charger of the late 1960s is remembered for its bold grille, hidden headlights and big engines. It became a screen star as well, which keeps its popularity high. Engines ranged from modest to very powerful, and the value of a car depends heavily on which one it has. Always ask for proof of the original engine.

From Wikipedia

The Dodge Charger is a mid-size automobile that was produced by Dodge from 1966 through 1978 model years, and was based on the Chrysler B platform.
