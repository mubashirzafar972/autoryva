Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

Who we are

Autoryva is a free guide to vintage and classic cars at https://autoryva.netlify.app. You can reach us through the feedback form on our About page.

What we collect

If you send us a message through the feedback form, we receive the message and the name or email you chose to type. We use it only to read and answer your feedback. Cars you save in the browser stay on your own device.

Cookies and similar technology

When you click Accept on our cookie notice, we may load Google Analytics to count visits and Google AdSense to show ads. These services may set cookies and collect information such as your device, pages viewed and approximate location. If you click Decline, we do not load them. You can clear your browser storage at any time to see the notice again.

Advertising

Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalised ads in your Google ad settings.

Other services

Our pages load car photos and text from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, fonts from Google Fonts, and are hosted by Netlify. These services receive your IP address when your browser loads their files and have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete a message you sent, and you can block cookies in your browser settings. We do not sell personal information.

Children

This site is a general interest site and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
